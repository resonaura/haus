# Repository baseline — 2026-11-04

This is a read-only baseline captured at `8c999d1` on branch `codex/platform`. It records what exists before the platform migration begins; it does not claim that an unrun build, test, device operation, or deployment passed.

## Architecture status at the baseline

The neutral `core` and `state` packages already exist, and the existing checker enforces their most important import boundaries. `@haus/ha` and `@haus/apps` also already exist. This is progress toward the new architecture, not proof that the migration is complete: Hub and Node still contain large legacy MJS composition code, the Hub keeps adapter re-export facades, and the checker currently scopes desktop, core, state and HA rather than the complete package graph.

The legacy architecture requirements at archived lines 11–14 are therefore **partially implemented**, while the publish/deployment requirement at line 28 remains **open**. The precise one-to-one requirement index is in [`docs/backlog/legacy.md`](../backlog/legacy.md).

The four checked extraction items at archived lines 7–10 are supported by current source: neutral records/runtime in `packages/core/src/{types,home,runtime}.ts`; HA wire/client/view implementations in `packages/ha/src/`; injected `HomeRuntime`/projection ports in `packages/state/src/index.ts`; and the HA projection strategy in `packages/ha/src/store.ts`. `packages/core/test/home.test.ts`, `packages/ha/test/adapter.test.ts` and shared lifecycle tests exercise provider isolation and canonical mapping. Their limited coverage does not establish complete HA capability conversion or a production Homebridge adapter.
